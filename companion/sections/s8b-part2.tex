\subsection{Part II: open at gate (II.11)}\label{sec:part2}

Nothing in Part~I depends on Part~II; \S14 of the record states that the two problems have
``no mathematical dependence in either direction'' \rec{\S14}{33}. Part~II asks a
different question: uniformity in a lattice or wavelet resolution, in the
operator-algebraic renormalisation programme over the wavelet maps of \cite{MMST}
\rec{\S1.3(d)}{4}, \rec{\S14}{33}. Its gate (II.11) asks that the lattice ground states,
evaluated on each fixed coarse Weyl generator, converge as the resolution is refined to
the vector state of the ground vector of a continuum torus operator constructed in phase
V3 (\texttt{part\_ii/gate\_II11\_scoping.pdf}, \S1); the scoping pass ``proves no part of
(II.11)''. Already for the free model, no finest-scale local-state-norm error of order
$\varepsilon_N^{\vartheta}$, $\vartheta>0$, is possible \rec{\S14}{33}, so the comparison
is made at a fixed coarse scale. Status per claim, verbatim from \texttt{docs/status.md}:
