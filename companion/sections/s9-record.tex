\section{The record and how to check it}\label{sec:record}

\emph{Every import is pinned to a page of its source; nothing here has been refereed.}

The record's repository is ``an experiment in open science'', and the work in it is
AI-assisted: the author drafted it with Anthropic's Claude as an interactive assistant
and published the working method, including the rounds that found errors in earlier
rounds' repairs (\texttt{README.md}, ``What this is''). Human verification is ongoing.

\emph{Gates.} The gate table of \texttt{docs/status.md} lists the Part~I gates, all
passed on 10~August 2026:
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
\item G0: it compiles; citations and references resolve; every import names its users;
\item G1/G2: each step of the two structured proof reconstructions maps to one environment
or display; no unmapped step, no orphan;
\item G3a/G3b: constants recomputed by hand; a forbidden-string check;
\item G4: citations and dossier entries correspond one to one; regression greps;
\item G5: three \texttt{pdflatex} passes, nothing unresolved, every page inspected;
\item G6: an adversarial pass; no confirmed finding, or every one fixed and re-checked.
\end{itemize}
The adversarial passes in \texttt{strategy.md}, which the README says the assistant ran,
are published.

\emph{Corrections.} The corrections history includes audit item P1 (a missing premise,
now (B4) of Hypothesis~13.1), S1--S3 (citation pinpoints: FKN transfer,
hypercontractivity, domain smoothing) and L1--L6 (local repairs, from a cell count to a
spectral-parameter range) \rec{Appendix~B}{35}, and gate R2 (the printed-norm prefactor,
read from the page image of GJS p.~594) \rec{Appendix~B}{34}. The scope amendment of
5~September 2026 gave $\UGJS$ (A8(vi)) the status described in \S\ref{sec:import}; ``No proof
in the manuscript changed'' \rec{title page}{1}.

\emph{C5.} Audit item C5 (\S\ref{sec:import}) is open \rec{Appendix~B}{35}: all nine
verdicts of \texttt{part\_i/\allowbreak C5\_reading\_record.md} are blank. It checks transcription,
not mathematics; closing it does not change the status of $\UGJS$
(\texttt{docs/status.md}).

\emph{How to check.} Every transcribed display of \texttt{part\_i/main.tex} carries a
\texttt{\% src:} comment naming its origin, and every import names its pinpoint source and
the page images documenting it in the audit dossier \rec{\S2.4}{7}. The page images are
third-party material and are not redistributed; \texttt{README.md} names a script that
regenerates those of the 16~August 2026 audit from a reader's own copies of the sources.
Statement and equation numbers here come from \texttt{part\_i/main.aux}.
