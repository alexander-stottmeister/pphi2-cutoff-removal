\section{The record and how to check it}\label{sec:record}

\emph{Every import is pinned to a page of its source; nothing here has been refereed.}

The record's repository is ``an experiment in open science'', and the work in it is
AI-assisted: the author drafted it with Anthropic's Claude as an interactive assistant
and published the working method, including the rounds that found errors in earlier
rounds' repairs (\texttt{README.md}, ``What this is''). Human verification is ongoing.

\emph{Gates.} The gate table of \texttt{docs/status.md} lists the Part~I gates, all
passed on 10~August 2026: G0 (it compiles), G1/G2 (each step of the two structured proof
reconstructions maps to the text), G3a/G3b (constants recomputed by hand), G4 (citations
match the dossier), G5 (three \texttt{pdflatex} passes, every page inspected), G6 (an
adversarial pass, every confirmed finding fixed). The adversarial passes in \texttt{strategy.md}, which the README says the assistant ran,
are published. Appendix~D's drafts and four audit rounds are in \texttt{ugjs/}, the gate
record of its integration in \texttt{part\_i/README.md} \recpp{title-page notice}{1--2}; that
record notes that the 110 pages were not each inspected and the dossier index not updated.

\emph{Corrections.} The corrections history includes audit item P1 (a missing premise,
now (B4) of Hypothesis~13.1), S1--S3 (citation pinpoints: FKN transfer,
hypercontractivity, domain smoothing), L1--L6 (local repairs, from a cell count to a
spectral-parameter range) and gate R2 (the printed-norm prefactor, read from the page
image of GJS p.~594) \rec{Appendix~B}{43}. The scope amendment of 5~September 2026 gave
$\UGJS$ (A8(vi)) the status of the source's own quantifier, still a named hypothesis; on
31~October 2026 A8(vi) was read off GJS Theorem~1.1.11; each time ``No proof in the
manuscript changed'' \rec{title page}{1}. On 7~November 2026 the integration of
Appendix~D retired A8(vi), listed A8(iv)-T, and changed the statements of Theorem~5.1 and
Corollary~5.2, which \Ttext{rest on A8(iv)-T}, and of Lemmas~7.1 and~7.6 (\S\ref{sec:import}); in the same pass it repaired
inputs of Appendix~C that were neither proved nor imported, a cell count shifted against
the GJS squares, which made the displayed bounds (7)--(8) false as stated (no theorem
affected), and the missing construction of the Wick powers in
$V(h)$ and $\mathcal U_h$ \rec{Appendix~B}{44}.

\emph{C5.} Audit item C5, a check of transcription, not of mathematics, is open: all
thirteen verdicts of \texttt{part\_i/\allowbreak C5\_reading\_record.md} are blank. Its
items 1--4 carry the imports of A8; items 10--11, 12(a),(c) and 9(c) ``carry the readings of
the Scope paragraph, points (b)--(d), and the item A8(iv)-T''; the rest is corroboration

\rec{Theorem~D.3, status}{91}.

\emph{How to check.} Every transcribed display of \texttt{part\_i/main.tex} carries a
\texttt{\% src:} comment naming its origin, and every import names its source and page
images in the audit dossier \rec{\S2.4}{9}; the images are third-party material, not
redistributed, and \texttt{README.md} names a script that regenerates those of the
16~August 2026 audit. Statement and equation numbers come from \texttt{part\_i/main.aux}.
