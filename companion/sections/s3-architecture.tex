\section{The architecture}\label{sec:architecture}

\emph{Two cutoff-uniform numbers, the gap $m_1$ and the moment $M$, make the net Cauchy.}

The table of the record's \S1.2 lays the proof out in steps \rec{\S1.2}{4}. Step~(i)
is the GJS input, the printed clustering theorem CE2 (Theorem~1.1.7, A8(iv)), including
its transverse case A8(iv)-T; step~(i$'$) derives cutoff-uniform moment bounds from it in
Appendices~C and~D (\S\ref{sec:import}). The Euclidean layer (the record's \S\S4--7)
derives the uniform local moment (M$_2$) and the uniform gap (G) by
Feynman--Kac--Nelson (FKN) formulas on slabs, steps (ii)--(iii); from these two the
Hamiltonian layer (its \S\S8--11) derives a local-perturbations-perturb-locally (LPPL)
estimate that makes the net Cauchy, with a rate, step~(iv); its \S12 shows that the limit
is a ground state of $(\A,\alpha)$ in the spectral sense (Proposition~12.1), with its
invariances, step~(v). The convergence argument is Hamiltonian; its two inputs come from
Euclidean estimates through slab FKN transfer.

Fig.~\ref{fig:spine} marks in orange what rests on A8(iv)-T, read from the record's proofs:
filled, a proof that uses it, directly (Theorem~D.4) or through the constant $M$
(Theorem~5.1 and every statement that consumes $M$); outlined, Proposition~12.1, whose
hypothesis (a sequence $(g_n)$ in $\G$ with $d(g_n,O)\to\infty$ for every bounded interval
$O$ and $\omega_{g_n}$ converging in norm on every local algebra $\A(O)$) only a filled
node supplies for $P(\varphi)_2$. Proposition~8.2 and Lemmas~9.3 and~10.1 are implications
under (G$_\gamma$) \rec{\S8}{30}: $\spec H(g)\subseteq\{0\}\cup[\gamma,\infty)$ and
$\dim\ker H(g)=1$ for every $g\in\CH$, which for $P(\varphi)_2$ only Theorem~7.8 supplies,
using Theorem~1.1.7 across parallel strips only.

Theorems~5.1 and~7.8 deliver the two numbers, which Theorem~12.2(5) restates for the
cutoff Hamiltonians $H(g)$, $g\in\CH$: the moment bound $\|V(h)\Om_g\|\le M$ for real
$h$ with $\|h\|_\infty\le1$ supported in an interval of length~$2$ \rec{Theorem~5.1, (M$_2$)}{24}, \Ttext{resting on A8(iv)-T} through
Theorem~D.4, and the gap $\spec H(g)\subseteq\{0\}\cup[m_1,\infty)$
\rec{Theorem~7.8, (G)}{30}.

The record's proofs show where each uniformity comes from. The $g$-independence of $m_1$
is CE2's printed $h$-independence \rec{Source Fact~2.2}{15}, applied across a horizontal
strip \rec{Lemma~7.4}{28}. In the record's \S7, Lemmas~7.1--7.3 use Theorem~D.3 at one
fixed $g$, uniformly in $T$ \recpp{Lemmas~7.1--7.3}{26--27}. Across $g$, Theorem~D.4
makes $M$ independent of $g$, at the price of transverse strips (\S\ref{sec:import}), and
Lemma~10.2 uses this at every cutoff $g_s$ of an affine path \rec{Lemma~10.2}{35}.

The record's \S\S8--10 work with an abstract gap $\gamma$ under (G$_\gamma$), and its
\S\S8--11 use the GJS package only through $m_1$ and $M$: Lemma~10.2 calls
Theorem~5.1, and $\gamma$ is instantiated as $m_1$ in \rec{\S11}{36}. Their output,
the one-path estimate \rec{Proposition~10.4, eq.~(27)}{35}, bounds
$\|(\omega_g-\omega_{g'})|_{\A(O)}\|$ whenever $g\equiv g'\equiv1$ on $O_d$,
$d\ge d_0:=4+2/\gamma$. Plateaus are cofinal and local duals are complete, so the net
converges in norm in each $\A(O)^*$, at a rate $\Psi_{m_1}$ faster than every inverse
power (\S\ref{sec:lppl}):
\begin{checkpoint}[Checkpoint: Theorem 11.1; through $M$, it rests on A8(iv)-T]
\begin{equation*}
 \|(\omega_g-\omega_\infty)|_{\A(O)}\|\le\Psi_{m_1}(d)
 \qquad\text{whenever }g\equiv1\text{ on }O_d .
 \tag*{\rec{Theorem~11.1, eq.~(31)}{36}}
\end{equation*}
\end{checkpoint}
