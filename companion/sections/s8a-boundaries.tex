\section{Boundaries, and Part II}\label{sec:boundaries}

\emph{Not unique, not sharp, not beyond weak coupling; and Part II is a different
question.}

\subsection{What is not claimed}\label{sec:notclaimed}

\emph{(a) Uniqueness.} Theorem~12.2, \condtext{conditional on $\UGJS$}, identifies the
limit of this cutoff family; it does not show that every locally normal ground state of
$(\A,\alpha)$, in the spectral sense of \rec{Proposition~12.1}{29}, equals
$\omega_\infty$ \rec{\S1.3(a)}{4}; \rec{\S14, \emph{Uniqueness}}{33}.

\emph{(b) Sharp rate.} $\Psi_{m_1}$ is super-polynomial but not exponential
\rec{\S1.3(b)}{4}. A proof of a sharp rate would require ``an additional resolvent or
Combes--Thomas input not supplied here'' \rec{\S14, \emph{Sharpness}}{33}. The record
reports, from its working note \texttt{m2/} (a proof sketch with numerics, outside the
document of record), that in the exactly solvable quadratic model the rate is
$\mathrm{poly}(d)\,e^{-2md}$, with $m$ that model's mass.

\emph{(c) Beyond weak coupling.} No claim is made outside
$0\le\lambda/m_0^2<\varepsilon_{\mathscr P}$ \rec{\S1.3(c)}{4}. Theorem~13.2 is
conditional on $\UGJS$ and on Hypothesis~13.1 \rec{Remark~13.3}{32};
\rec{\S14, \emph{Beyond weak coupling}}{33}, whose four items
(B1)--(B4) are all load-bearing \rec{\S13}{31}. For even $\mathscr P$ at weak coupling,
(B1)--(B4) cannot all hold; the proof uses the uniform gap through Lemma~9.3 and
Theorem~12.2 (1), (3), (4), hence also (M$_2$) and $\UGJS$.

\emph{(d) Not addressed.} A gap for a GNS Hamiltonian of $\omega_\infty$ is not
addressed: item (5) concerns the cutoff Hamiltonians $H(g)$, $g\in\CH$. Nor is
$\omega_\infty$ identified with the Euclidean (Wightman) theory of GJS; no reverse
Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction is used \rec{abstract}{2}. Lorentz covariance is not
discussed.
