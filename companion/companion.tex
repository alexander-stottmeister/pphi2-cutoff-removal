\begin{abstract}
\noindent A reading guide to the record of 7 November 2026 on the spatially cutoff
$P(\varphi)_2$ model; it proves nothing new. The record's Theorem~12.2 states that, for
$0\le\lambda/m_0^2<\varepsilon_{\mathscr P}$, the full net of cutoff ground states
$\omega_g$ (no subnets, no averaging) converges in norm on every local algebra $\A(O)$,
at a super-polynomial rate in the distance to the plateau edge; the limit is
locally normal, translation and (for even $\mathscr P$) $\Z_2$ invariant, and a ground state
of the Glimm--Jaffe dynamics in the spectral sense. It is proved from printed statements,
with one Glimm--Jaffe--Spencer theorem imported, used also in a case its printed statement
covers and its printed proof does not reach; C5's human readings (items 1--4, 9(c), 10--12)
are open.
We explain why the question was open, how a uniform gap and moment and an exact light
cone replace compactness, and what is not claimed.
\end{abstract}
