\begin{abstract}
\noindent A reading guide to a 40-page research record on the spatially cutoff
$P(\varphi)_2$ model; it proves nothing new. The record's Theorem~12.2 states that, for
$0\le\lambda/m_0^2<\varepsilon_{\mathscr P}$, the full net of cutoff ground states $\omega_g$ (plateau
order, no subnets, no averaging) converges in norm on every local algebra $\A(O)$. The rate
is faster than every inverse power of the distance from $O$ to the plateau edge. The limit
is locally normal, translation and (for even $\mathscr P$) $\Z_2$ invariant, and a ground state of
the Glimm--Jaffe dynamics in the spectral sense. The theorem is proved conditional on
$\UGJS$, a cutoff-uniform Glimm--Jaffe--Spencer moment estimate. The record imports it at
the scope at which the source states and uses it, without re-deriving it. We explain
why the question was open, how a uniform gap and an exact light cone replace compactness,
and what is not claimed.
\end{abstract}
