#### Lemma 10.1 (state derivative)

`lem:statederiv` &middot; page 34 &middot; proof in the manuscript


#### Lemma 10.5 (the rate function is super-polynomial)

`lem:rate` &middot; page 36 &middot; imports A8 &middot; proof in the manuscript

```latex
Define
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_\gamma(d):=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle
 32M\sum_{n\ge\lfloor d\rfloor}
 \Bigl(\|W_\gamma\|_1e^{-\gamma n/4}+T_\gamma(n/2)\Bigr), & d\ge d_0,\\[6pt]
 \max\Bigl\{2,\ \text{the same expression}\Bigr\}, & 0\le d<d_0 .
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:Psidef}

\begin{equation}
 \Psi_\gamma(d)\le C_{N,\gamma,M}\,(1+d)^{-N}\qquad(d\ge0) .
 \label{eq:Psitail}
\end{equation}
In particular $\|(\omega_g-\omega_{g'})|_{\A(O)}\|\le\Psi_\gamma(d)$ whenever
$g\equiv g'\equiv1$ on $O_d$, $d\ge0$.
```

### Ground state, invariances, and the Main Theorem

#### Proposition 12.1 (exact spectral passage)

`prop:spectral` &middot; page 37 &middot; proof in the manuscript

```latex
If $(g_n)\subseteq\G$ satisfies $d(g_n,O)\to\infty$ for every bounded interval $O$, and
$\omega_{g_n}\to\omega$ in norm on every $\A(O)$, then $\omega$ is a ground state of
$(\A,\alpha)$ in the spectral sense: $\omega\circ\alpha_t=\omega$ for all $t$; the
function $t\mapsto\omega(A\alpha_t(B))$ is continuous and bounded for all $A,B\in\A$; and
\[
 \int_\R f(t)\,\omega(A\alpha_t(B))\,dt=0
\]
for every $f\in L^1(\R)$ whose transform $\check f(E)=\int f(t)e^{itE}\,dt$ vanishes on
$[0,\infty)$.
```

Provenance: `src: m6/main.tex prop:M0-main(i) (v2), transcribed; = lamport_gap_to_main.tex <1>10.`

#### Theorem 12.2 (Main Theorem)

`thm:MAIN` &middot; page 38 &middot; imports A8 &middot; proof in the manuscript

```latex
Let $0\le\lambda/m_0^2<\varepsilon_{\mathscr P}$. Then there is a state $\omega_\infty$ on
$\A$ such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\rm(1)] \emph{(full-net convergence with rate)} for every bounded open interval $O$,
      $\lim_{g\in\G}\|(\omega_g-\omega_\infty)|_{\A(O)}\|=0$, and
      $\|(\omega_g-\omega_\infty)|_{\A(O)}\|\le\Psi_{m_1}(d)$ whenever $g\equiv1$ on $O_d$,
      with $\Psi_{m_1}$ decaying faster than every inverse power;
\item[\rm(2)] \emph{(local normality)} $\omega_\infty|_{\A(O)}$ is normal for every $O$;
\item[\rm(3)] \emph{(ground state)} $\omega_\infty$ is a ground state of $(\A,\alpha)$ in the
      spectral sense;
\item[\rm(4)] \emph{(invariances)} $\omega_\infty\circ\tau_a=\omega_\infty$ for all $a\in\R$;
      and for even $\mathscr P$, $\omega_\infty\circ\theta=\omega_\infty$;
\item[\rm(5)] \emph{(spectra and local moment)}
      \[
       \spec H(g)\subseteq\{0\}\cup[m_1,\infty)\quad(g\in\CH),
       \qquad \dim\ker H(g)=1,
      \]
      and $\sup_{g\in\CH}\|V(h)\Om_g\|\le M$ for real $h$ with $\|h\|_\infty\le1$ supported
      in an interval of length $2$.
\end{enumerate}
The weak-coupling estimate imported is one printed theorem of \cite{GJS}: Theorem 1.1.7
(Source Fact~\ref{sf:CE2}), used at its printed scope, including the transverse case
```

### The two-phase dichotomy

#### Hypothesis 13.1 (structure of a broken phase)

`hyp:broken` &middot; page 39

```latex
$\mathscr P$ is even, and:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\rm(B1)] there are translation-invariant ground states $\omega_+$ and $\omega_-$ of
      $(\A,\alpha)$ such that, for all bounded local $X,Y$,
      \[
       \omega_\pm(X\tau_a(Y))-\omega_\pm(X)\,\omega_\pm(\tau_a(Y))
       \longrightarrow0\qquad(|a|\to\infty);
      \]
\item[\rm(B2)] $\omega_+\circ\theta=\omega_-$ and $\omega_-\circ\theta=\omega_+$;
\item[\rm(B3)] there are a bounded local observable $B=B^*\in\A(O)$ and $m\ne0$ such that
      $\omega_\pm(B)=\pm m$;
\item[\rm(B4)] every $\theta$-invariant ground state of $(\A,\alpha)$ equals
      $\tfrac12(\omega_++\omega_-)$.
\end{enumerate}
```

#### Assumption A1 (cutoff construction)

`A:cutoff` &middot; page 9 &middot; imports A8

```latex
For every $g\in\CH$: $K(g)=H_0+V(g)$ is essentially self-adjoint and bounded below; its closure
(again denoted $K(g)$) has $E(g)=\inf\spec K(g)$ as a \emph{simple} eigenvalue, and the
normalized eigenvector $\Om_g$ may be chosen strictly positive $d\phi_0$-a.e.\ in $Q$-space.
For every real $h\in L^1\cap L^2$ of compact support, $V(h)$ is self-adjoint on its maximal
multiplication domain.

\emph{Wick powers of the time-zero field} (added 7 November 2026; the construction behind
$V(h)$ of \S\ref{subsec:model}).  Let $\HH_C$ be the real Hilbert space obtained by
completing the real-valued $\mathcal S(\R)$ in the inner product $2\ip f{Cg}$ ($C$ is bounded and
strictly positive, so this is an inner product, and $\mathcal S(\R)$ is a dense continuously
imbedded subspace, $2\ip f{Cf}\le\|f\|_2^2/m_0$).  $Q$-space with $d\phi_0$ and $\varphi_0$ is
the Gaussian random process indexed by $\HH_C$ in the source's normalisation, ``(c)
$\langle\phi(v)\phi(w)\rangle=\frac12\langle v,w\rangle$, the inner product on $\mathcal H$''
\cite[Definition, p.~15]{SimonBook}: $(\varphi_0(f))_f$ is a centred Gaussian family with
$\E[\varphi_0(f)\varphi_0(g)]=\ip f{Cg}$; such a process is determined by its index space up to
isomorphism \cite[Thm.~I.6, p.~15]{SimonBook}.  The
source's definitions are used as printed.
\emph{(a)}~For Gaussian random variables $f_1,\dots,f_k$, ``The Wick product
${:}f_1^{n_1}\cdots f_k^{n_k}{:}$ is defined recursively in $n\equiv n_1+\dots+n_k$ by
${:}f_1^0\cdots f_k^0{:}=1$, $\langle{:}f_1^{n_1}\cdots f_k^{n_k}{:}\rangle=0\,(n\ne0)$ and
$\frac{\partial}{\partial f_i}{:}f_1^{n_1}\cdots f_k^{n_k}{:}=n_i\,{:}f_1^{n_1}\cdots
f_i^{n_i-1}\cdots f_k^{n_k}{:}$'' \cite[p.~12]{SimonBook}; for one variable,
${:}f^n{:}$ is the coefficient of $\alpha^n/n!$ in ${:}\exp(\alpha f){:}=\exp(\alpha
f-\frac12\alpha^2\langle f^2\rangle)$ \cite[(I.15), (I.18a), p.~10]{SimonBook}, explicitly
${:}f^n{:}=\sum_{m=0}^{[n/2]}\frac{n!}{m!\,(n-2m)!}\,f^{n-2m}\bigl(-\tfrac12\langle
f^2\rangle\bigr)^m$ \cite[(I.18b), p.~10]{SimonBook}.
\emph{(b)}~$\langle{:}f^n{:}\;{:}g^m{:}\rangle=\delta_{nm}\,n!\,\langle fg\rangle^n$ for
jointly Gaussian random variables $f,g$ \cite[Thm.~I.3, (I.21), p.~11]{SimonBook} (its proof
uses that $\alpha f+\beta g$ is Gaussian; every use below is within one Gaussian process), and
$\langle{:}f_1\cdots f_n{:}\;{:}g_1\cdots g_m{:}\rangle=0$ if $n\ne m$
\cite[Cor.~I.4, (I.23a), p.~12]{SimonBook}.
\emph{(c)}~$\Gamma_n(\HH)$ is ``the closed subspace[\,] generated by
$\{{:}\phi(f_1)\cdots\phi(f_n){:}\mid f_i\in\mathcal H\}$'' \cite[p.~16]{SimonBook} (p.~17
says ``spanned by''), with $\Gamma(\HH)=L^2(d\phi_0)$ for $\HH=\HH_C$; by (b) the spaces
```

#### Lemma 9.1 (exact inverse-energy filter)

`lem:filter` &middot; page 32 &middot; proof in the manuscript

```latex

 \label{eq:filterdef}
\end{equation}

\emph{(i)} $W_\gamma\in L^1(\R)$ and, for every $q\ge0$,
$\int_\R(1+|t|)^q|W_\gamma(t)|\,dt<\infty$; in particular

 T_\gamma(r):=\int_{|t|>r}|W_\gamma(t)|\,dt
 \le C_{q,\gamma}(1+r)^{-q}\qquad(q\ge0,\ r\ge0).

\emph{(ii)} If $H$ is self-adjoint with
```

#### Lemma C.3 (Wick lower bound; the $h\ge0$ entry)

`lem:wick` &middot; page 49 &middot; proof in the manuscript

```latex
With
\begin{gather*}
 C(\mathscr P):=\sum_{n=0}^{2p'}|a_n|\max_{0\le j\le n/2}\frac{n!}{j!\,(n-2j)!\,2^j},\\
 C_{p'}:=\max\Bigl\{1,\ \max_{1\le m<2p'}\frac{2p'-m}{2p'}\,(2m)^{m/(2p'-m)}\Bigr\},
\end{gather*}
\[
 b(\mathscr P):=C_{p'}\sum_{m=0}^{2p'-1}C(\mathscr P)^{2p'/(2p'-m)}\,a_{2p'}^{-m/(2p'-m)} ,
\]
one has ${:}\mathscr P{:}_c(x)\ge-b(\mathscr P)\,(1+c)^{p'}$ for all $c\ge0$ and $x\in\R$.
Consequently, for every $h\in\CE$ with $\supp h\subseteq\Lambda$ and every $\kappa\ge2$,
\[
  \mathcal U_{h,\kappa}\ \ge\
  -\lambda\,b(\mathscr P)\,|\Lambda|\,\bigl(1+C_*+\tfrac1{2\pi}\log\kappa\bigr)^{p'}
  \ \ge\ -c_1\,(\log\kappa)^{p'},
\]
\[
  c_1:=\max\Bigl\{1,\ \lambda\,b(\mathscr P)\,|\Lambda|\Bigl(\frac{1+C_*}{\log2}+\frac1{2\pi}
  \Bigr)^{p'}\Bigr\}>0 ,
\]
with $C_*$ of \eqref{eq:ckappa}; $c_1$ depends on $\lambda$, $\mathscr P$, $|\Lambda|$, $m_0$
and $\|\delta\|_\infty$ (the last two through $C_*$), and the maximum with $1$ makes
$c_1\ge1$, which the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:stability} uses (for its threshold $b_1$).
```

#### Lemma C.4 (ultraviolet difference; the $h\le1$ entry)

`lem:uv` &middot; page 49 &middot; proof in the manuscript

