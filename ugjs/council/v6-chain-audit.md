### 3.1 Findings F-A to F-K

**F-A. Corollary 12.3: the statement exceeds the proof (the cutoff of [GJ3]). Severity: CRITICAL
for the statement of Corollary 12.3 as written; MINOR for the record, because no result uses the
corollary.**
- *Locator:* `cor:canonical`, Corollary 12.3, p. 42, ll. 3126-3160. Source: [GJ3] printed p. 210,
  PDF page 8 of the GJ3 file in `refs/`, image `audit_2026/evidence/gjiii-08.png`; printed p. 205,
  PDF page 3 (rendered at 150 dpi).
- *Text:* statement, ll. 3128-3130: "Every $w^*$ limit point of the (averaged or unaveraged)
  finite-volume ground states constructed in \cite[\S2]{GJ3} coincides on each $\A(O)$ with
  $\omega_\infty$". Proof, l. 3136: "fix $g\in\G$ with $g\equiv1$ on $[-3,3]$".
- *Source:* GJ3 p. 210 prints "For concreteness, we take g(x) to be a nonnegative C_0^∞ function
  equal to one on the interval [−3, 3]". p. 205 prints "a smooth, positive function that equals one
  on some bounded set and that vanishes off some larger bounded set". Neither page requires g ≤ 1,
  while G requires 0 ≤ g ≤ 1 (Definition 2.1, l. 571).
- *Why it fails:* for a cutoff that GJ3 admits with max g > 1, the functions g_n = g(·/n) are
  outside C_H. A1 is imported only for g ∈ C_H, and Theorems 5.1 and 7.8, Proposition 10.4 and
  Theorem 12.2(1) are stated for C_H or G. Nothing in the record reaches ω_{g_n} or ω_n for such g.
  For g ∈ G I re-derived every line and the proof is correct.
- *Repair:* add to the statement "with the cutoff $g$ of \cite[(2.3)]{GJ3} chosen in $\G$, i.e.
  $0\le g\le1$". The alternative, extending the estimates to cutoffs above 1, is not available in
  the record.

- *Point 3, the reading:* the corollary does not name that reading.

  Assumption~\ref{A:cutoff}, Scope, for \cite[(1.10)]{GJ3}".

**F-D. Hypothesis 13.1 does not say in which sense "ground state" is meant; a quantifier is
loose. MINOR.**
- *Locator:* (B1), l. 3176, "translation-invariant ground states"; (B4), l. 3185, "every
  $\theta$-invariant ground state of $(\A,\alpha)$"; proof, l. 3201, "Fix $a\in\R$ such that …",
  then l. 3230, "$\xrightarrow[\ |a|\to\infty\ ]{}0$".
- *Why it fails, the sense of "ground state":* Step 3 applies (B4) to ω_∞. Theorem 12.2(3) makes
  ω_∞ a ground state only "in the spectral sense" of Proposition 12.1. Suppose (B4) is read with
  another notion, for example the generator definition for C*-dynamical systems. That notion
  presupposes a strong continuity of α which the record does not establish, and Step 3 would then
  need a property of ω_∞ that is not proved.
- *Why it fails, the quantifier:* Steps 1-2 hold for every a with R_a > 0 and m_1R_a ≥ 1. For
  O = (c, c+L) these are the a with \|a\| ≥ L + 1/m_1, a nonempty set.
- *Repair:* write "ground state … in the spectral sense of Proposition~\ref{prop:spectral}" in (B1)
  and (B4). Write "For every $a$ with …" in place of "Fix $a$".

**F-E. Section 1.3(c) and its echoes ascribe to the gap alone what the proof gets from the gap and

- *Locator:* §1.3(c), ll. 320-323: "a cutoff-uniform gap is incompatible with that two-phase

- *Why it fails:* Theorem 13.2 is stated only for 0 ≤ λ/m_0² < ε_𝒫. Its Steps 2-3 use Theorem

  valid, but it is not a numbered result.
