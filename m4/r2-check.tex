\begin{abstract}
Gate R2 concerned the prefactor of the GJS norms (1.1.7)--(1.1.8), illegible in the OCR text
layer of the JSTOR scan. It is closed by rendering p.~594 of the scan as a 300\,dpi image and
reading the print directly. The printed norms are
\[
  \|Q\|=K_1\Bigl[\prod_\Delta K_2^{2N(\Delta)}\,N(\Delta)!\Bigr]\|w\|_2,
  \qquad
  \|Q(x_0)\|=K_1\Bigl[\prod_\Delta K_2^{2N(\Delta)}\,N(\Delta)!\Bigr]\|w(x_0,\cdot)\|_2 .
\]
Two findings. \textbf{(i)} $K_1$ enters to the \emph{first} power; the exponent ambiguity is
resolved as $e_*=1$. \textbf{(ii)} The local weight is $K_2^{2N(\Delta)}$, \emph{not}
$K_2^{N(\Delta)}$: the OCR had dropped the factor $2$. Consequently the constant correction
``$K_2^{4p}\to K_2^{2p}$'' made in \texttt{m2-audit.tex}, Remark~5.3(i), was itself an OCR
artifact and is \textbf{withdrawn}; the original $K_2^{4p'}$ form of
\texttt{weak-coupling-strategy.tex} is restored. The cell-count correction $32\to9$ stands
(it was geometry, not OCR). All finiteness and uniformity statements of the programme are
unaffected --- the exponent never entered any qualitative conclusion --- but every explicit
constant is now exact; the corrected values are propagated through \texttt{m6/main.tex} and
listed here, including $M^2=9\cdot8!\,C_8K_1K_2^{16}\lambda^2$ for
$\mathscr P(\xi)=\xi^4$. The recomputation also caught a second, independent slip in the v1
manuscript --- a half-degree error ($(2p')!$ for $(4p')!$, $K_2^{4p'}$ for $K_2^{8p'}$) predating
R2 --- now fixed in \texttt{m6/main.tex}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Method and evidence}

The JSTOR scan's embedded text layer garbles the displays (1.1.7)--(1.1.8) of \cite{GJS}
(``\texttt{K1[IaK21N(a)}\dots''), which forced the parametric treatment
$K_1^{e_*}\prod K_2^{N(\Delta)}N(\Delta)!$, $e_*\in\{1,r,\sum N(\Delta)\}$, in
\texttt{m2-audit.tex}. Rendering PDF page 11 ($=$ printed p.~594) at 300\,dpi
(\texttt{pdftoppm}) makes the print unambiguous:

\begin{srcfact}[p.~594, displays (1.1.7)--(1.1.9), read from the page image]\label{sf:norm}
\[
  \textup{(1.1.7)}\quad
  \|Q\|=K_1\Bigl[\prod_\Delta K_2^{2N(\Delta)}N(\Delta)!\Bigr]\|w\|_2,
  \qquad
  \textup{(1.1.8)}\quad
  \|Q(x_0)\|=K_1\Bigl[\prod_\Delta K_2^{2N(\Delta)}N(\Delta)!\Bigr]\|w(x_0,\cdot)\|_2,
\]
\[
  \textup{(1.1.9)}\quad \|Q\|=\sum\nolimits_l\|Q_l\|\ \ \text{(sum over localized pieces)} .
\]
\end{srcfact}

\begin{srcfact}[consistency cross-checks]\label{sf:cross}
(a) p.~614 (rendered likewise): the factors $\mathcal K=\mathcal K_1\mathcal K_2\mathcal K_3
\mathcal K_4$ and the category weights $K_1^\nu$, $\nu\in\{1,\dots,6\}$ (Table~1), belong to the
\emph{Chapter-2} norm $(2.4.3)$, not to (1.1.7)--(1.1.8); the Remark preceding Theorem~2.4.1
relates the two by $|\!|\!|m_\alpha|\!|\!|=K_1^\nu e^{\tau t}\|m_\alpha\|_0$ for initial and
final terms, which is dimensionally consistent only with a \emph{single} power of $K_1$ inside
$\|\cdot\|_0$. (b) $\gamma=(n+1)m_0(1-\varepsilon)$ is printed on p.~614, matching the decay
weight used in the imported machinery. (c) Footnote 9 (p.~593) is unchanged: $\kappa=\infty$ is
admissible at the price $N(\Delta)!\to(N(\Delta)!)^3$; no $K$-factors are affected.
\end{srcfact}

\section{Corrected constants}

Throughout, $Q=\prod_{i=1}^r\varphi_0(f_i)$ with $\supp f_i$ in a length-$L$ interval, and the
localization sum uses at most $L+1$ cells per factor. Since every point has $m_i=1$ and
$\sum_\Delta N(\Delta)$ equals the number of points, the $K_2$-weight of any localized piece is
$K_2^{2\cdot\#\text{points}}$ --- additive and independent of how points share cells.

\begin{proposition}[exact norm bounds]\label{prop:norms}
\begin{align*}
  \|Q\|_{\rm GJS}&\le K_1K_2^{2r}\,r!\prod_{i=1}^r\sqrt{L+1}\,\|f_i\|_2, &
  \|Q^2\|_{\rm GJS}&\le K_1K_2^{4r}\,(2r)!\prod_{i=1}^r(L+1)\|f_i\|_2^2 ,\\
  \intertext{and for the interaction square of \textup{(M$_2$)} (two points, degrees
  $n,n'\le\deg\mathscr P=2p'$, hence $n+n'\le4p'$; $\le9$ cell pairs):}
  \|Q_V\|&\le 9\,\mathfrak K(p')\,\lambda^2\Bigl(\sum_n|a_n|\Bigr)^2, &
  \mathfrak K(p')&=K_1K_2^{8p'}\,(4p')! \,.
\end{align*}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from Source Fact~\ref{sf:norm}: $\prod_\Delta K_2^{2N(\Delta)}=K_2^{2\sum N(\Delta)}$,
$\prod_\Delta N(\Delta)!\le(\sum N(\Delta))!$, Cauchy--Schwarz over $\le L+1$ cells per factor,
and, for $Q_V$: $\sum_\Delta N(\Delta)=n+n'\le4p'$, so
$\prod_\Delta K_2^{2N(\Delta)}=K_2^{2(n+n')}\le K_2^{8p'}$ and
$\prod_\Delta N(\Delta)!\le(n+n')!\le(4p')!$, in both cell configurations (shared or distinct
squares --- the exponent is additive).
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[(M$_2$) constant, exact form]\label{cor:M2}
$\displaystyle\sup_{g}\|V(h)\Om_g\|^2\le M^2=9\,C_8\,K_1K_2^{8p'}(4p')!\,\lambda^2
\Bigl(\sum_{n=0}^{2p'}|a_n|\Bigr)^2$. For $\mathscr P(\xi)=\xi^4$ ($p'=2$, $\sum|a_n|=1$;
degrees exactly $4{+}4=8$):
\[
  M^2=9\cdot8!\;C_8K_1K_2^{16}\lambda^2=362\,880\;C_8K_1K_2^{16}\lambda^2 .
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[exponential-moment radius, slight improvement]\label{cor:dense}
$\E_{\mu_g}[\varphi_0(f)^{2n}]\le C_8K_1\,(K')^{2n}(2n)!$ with $K'=K_2^{2}\sqrt{L+1}\|f\|_2$:
the prefactor $K_1$ now sits \emph{outside} the geometric base, so the radius of exponential
integrability $s_0=1/K'$ is independent of $K_1$ --- marginally stronger than the v1 statement,
where $K_1$ had to be absorbed into the base to cover all readings.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[a correction corrected]\label{rem:mea}
\texttt{m2-audit.tex}, Remark~5.3(i), ``corrected'' the constant of
\texttt{weak-coupling-strategy.tex} from $K_2^{4p}$ to $K_2^{2p}$. That step trusted the OCR
reading $K_2^{N(\Delta)}$ and is hereby withdrawn: with the printed weight $K_2^{2N(\Delta)}$,
the \emph{original} $K_2^{4p'}$ was correct. The other half of that remark --- the cell count
$32\to9$ for supports of length $2$ --- is geometric and stands. Net constant versus the
original strategy claim: $9/32$ of it, with the factorial and $K_2$-exponent now tracking
$\deg\mathscr P$ correctly. (The same recomputation exposed a half-degree slip
--- $(2p')!$, $K_2^{4p'}$ in place of $(4p')!$, $K_2^{8p'}$ --- that had entered
\texttt{m6/main.tex} at the v0 assembly through a $p$-versus-$2p'$ convention change between
\texttt{m2-audit.tex} ($p=\deg\mathscr P$) and \texttt{m6} ($2p'=\deg\mathscr P$); fixed.)
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[what R2 did and did not affect]\label{rem:scope}
No statement of \texttt{m6/main.tex} changes its truth value: finiteness and $g$-uniformity of
(M$_2$), the gap $m_1$ in (G), the rate function $\Psi_{m_1}$, and all conclusions of the Main
Theorem are exponent-insensitive, as asserted throughout. What R2 closes is the \emph{exact
form} of every displayed constant; \texttt{m6/main.tex} has been updated in place (norm
description in \S3, Theorem 4.1's $\mathfrak K$, the (G)-section norm bounds, the density-proof
radius, the interface checklist). The remaining indeterminacy is irreducible at citation level:
GJS fix $m_0,K_2,K_1$ ``sufficiently large'' and $\lambda$ ``sufficiently small'' without
numerical values, so all constants are explicit \emph{in terms of} $(K_1,K_2,C_7,C_8,m_1)$ and
cannot be more.
\end{remark}
